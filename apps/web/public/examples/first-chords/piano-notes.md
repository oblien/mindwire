# First chords

A short introduction to the piano.

## Meet the keys
The white keys repeat C, D, E, F, G, A, B. Find C just before a pair of black keys. Play each note and listen to how the pitch changes.

## Build a C major chord
Play C, E, and G together. These are the root, major third, and perfect fifth of C major. Try the notes separately, then together.

## Check your understanding
1. Which three notes make a C major chord? C, E, and G.
2. Which white key comes immediately after C? D.
