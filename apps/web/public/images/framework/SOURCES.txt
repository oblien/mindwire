Mindwire Signal Studio — October 31, 2026

The SVG illustrations, line icons, central relay, wire sculptures, cursor
ribbon, and five signal-field posters in this directory are original,
project-authored vector drawings. They are generated by:
  apps/web/scripts/generate-signal-art.ts
  apps/web/components/landing/effects/signal-field.ts

Regenerate with `bun scripts/generate-signal-art.ts` from apps/web.

Visual direction: folded knowledge folios, layered product surfaces,
modular tool ports, continuous signal paths, coral / periwinkle / sage
accents, and a rounded Mindwire relay. No reference-site vector paths,
embedded images, traced screenshots, or raster textures are used in these
illustrations. Artwork is authored directly in SVG; no image-generation
model or prompt was used.

The previous public exports from rainbrand.com were replaced. Their unused
agent-row illustration, raster textures, extracted shader, and scene data
were removed. This replacement covers the artwork; the site's existing
page shell, typography, and shared rounded-surface geometry remain.

Provider marks in /logos identify their respective services and retain
those brands' identities. The agent rack uses independent links and the
project's existing standalone marks. codex-color.svg is derived from the
existing normalized codex.svg, rather than the former six-icon export.

Fonts remain in public/fonts/framework with their accompanying OFL licenses.
